Graveyard Night · Maaddism Productions

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

In short

Graveyard Night does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game works entirely on your phone, with no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Graveyard Night is made by Maaddism Productions. This policy covers the Graveyard Night app for Android. You can contact us at daretoai@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The name you choose to play under, your chapter progress, scores, coins, upgrades and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can remove them at any time with Settings > Erase progress, or by uninstalling the game.

What we do not do

We do not ask for or read your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

The game shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and has no sign-in. It does not send information over the internet.

There is nothing to buy in the game. Coins are earned only by playing.

Google Play

When you download or update the game, Google Play handles that under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive personal information about you from Google Play.

Children

Graveyard Night is a horror game intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding purchases or ads, we will update this policy and the date below before that version is released.

Contact
